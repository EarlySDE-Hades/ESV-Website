    Life at Earlyseed

    October 2025

    From task lists to ideas on the board

    A few years ago most of our meetings ended with a list of tasks. Now they end with 15 to 20 new ideas on the board.

    What
    How we run meetings
    Where
    Our office, Mumbai

    What changed

    Ownership. Monica Gupta, our co-founder, stopped arriving with all the answers once it was clear the team had answers of its own. These days she often leaves with better ideas than the ones she brought.

    Why it works

    Google’s Project Aristotle studied more than 180 teams. The best sign that a team would do well was its environment, and whether people felt safe to share, argue and try things.

    What we see

    • Decisions are made faster.
    • People are more engaged.
    • Once the direction is set, the team works out the rest.
