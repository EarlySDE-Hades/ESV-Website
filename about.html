    About us

    Boutique investment banking for India’s high-potential start-ups.

    Earlyseed Ventures is a boutique investment banking firm based in Mumbai. We work closely with fundamentally strong, tech-based B2B and B2C start-ups creating immense value, and help them raise seed, pre-Series A and Series A rounds.

    Pitch to us Meet the team

    Our mission

    Being instrumental in the making of a new India: a self-sufficient and self-reliant India.

    Our story

    Built as a bridge between young, growing businesses and private equity investors, we build genuine financial partnerships.

    Earlyseed Ventures started in 2020 as a consultancy for angel investors who wanted to expand their portfolio by investing in a new asset class: startup funding.

    We are now on a mission to create an ecosystem of transformational mentorship for founders at every level, from universities to incubation centres to big-ticket fund seekers.

    Our commitment to founders is one of authentic partnership. We get into the skin of the business. Once due diligence is complete and a startup is taken on, we walk to the end of the path, which is why we analyse our parameters of entry closely before we begin.

    How we work

    For start-ups

    1. Round one

      Fill in the starter form and send us your pitch deck. We get back to you in 7 to 10 working days.

    2. Deep dive

      A 1:1 session to get to know the founders and the brand: the core values and ideas that drive the business.

    3. Building a funding strategy

      We understand your funding requirements and build with you a pitch deck that captures your market position, the potential of the idea and the strength of the team behind it.

    4. Mandate and meetings

      Once you are convinced to work with us, we agree on a mandate and start setting up meetings with aligned investors.

    Pitch to us

    For investors

    1. Deep dive

      We learn your investment philosophy and strategy.

    2. Knowledge sharing

      We share our view of the startup ecosystem, then present curated start-ups to look at, meet and potentially invest in.

    3. Closing the loop

      If you commit, we conduct due procedures to close the investment and lay out the next steps for founders and investors.

    4. Sustainable partnership

      Startup investments are slow-brewing. We are here for the long haul, working with you on a regular basis.

    Our growth journey

    1. 2020

      Began our association as regional partners for a venture capital fund.

    2. 2022

      Earlyseed Ventures Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated to support start-ups through investment banking services.

    3. 2023

      Established an angel investor syndicate and carried out investments under the Earlyseed Ventures banner.

    4. 2024

      Onboarded venture partners in Ludhiana and Jaipur, and conducted bootcamps in collaboration with Manipal.

    5. 2025

      Launched the pre-IPO vertical and onboarded two franchise partners.

    6. 2026 Goal
