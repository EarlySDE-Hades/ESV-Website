    Kyoora

    Kyoora is building the operating system for India’s pharmaceutical distribution. India’s pharma supply chain is among the most fragmented in the world: thousands of drug manufacturers reach lakhs of pharmacies through tens of thousands of regional distributors, and a typical pharmacy counter deals with many distributors at once. Kyoora addresses this by acquiring and operating regional distributors and integrating them on proprietary software that it builds and owns. Each distributor added to the network strengthens the whole, creating a single, compounding system that connects manufacturers, distributors and pharmacies. By consolidating and digitising the middle of the supply chain, Kyoora aims to improve availability, efficiency and reliability in how medicines move across India, from the factory to the pharmacy shelf.
