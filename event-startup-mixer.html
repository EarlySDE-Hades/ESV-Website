    The Startup Mixer comes to BKC

    An evening in Mumbai where founders sit across the table from investors who can back them, advisors who have seen what works, and others building companies of their own.

    Event
    The Startup Mixer
    Where
    BKC, Mumbai
    Our part
    Co-host

    The evening

    We co-hosted the Mixer with Global St. Angelo’s Venture Funding and Vis Legis Law Practice at Santé Spa Cuisine, from 4 to 6 pm. Seats were limited and the guest list was curated, so the conversations had room to go somewhere.

    What it is for

    • Conversations about capital, with people ready to invest.
    • Clarity on fundraising and compliance.
    • Connections that help with the next phase of growth.
    • Time with other founders.

    Next editions

    The Mixer is recurring, and Edition 5 is set for 2 December 2026 at the same venue. Details, and later dates, are under upcoming events.

    Great ideas, good advice and the right capital belong in one room.
