    The Startup Mixer: capital, connections and growth

    An evening in Mumbai where founders sit across the table from investors who can back them, advisors who have seen what works, and others building companies of their own.

    Event
    The Startup Mixer
    Where
    BKC and Goregaon, Mumbai
    Our part
    Co-host

    Conversation instead of speeches

    Most start-up events put a few people on a stage and everyone else in rows. The Mixer does the opposite. There are no long speeches and no crowded pitching, only two hours of conversation in a small room.

    A structured format moves people past the usual round of names and business cards, so a founder can talk properly with an investor, a lawyer or another founder. The guest list is curated and the organisers confirm each place, which keeps the room small and relevant.

    Who it is for

    • Founders preparing to raise, or growing after a raise.
    • Angel investors, venture funds and family offices.
    • Legal, financial and transaction advisors.
    • Incubators, accelerators and corporate innovation teams.
    • Business owners looking at start-ups as partners.

    What people talk about

    • Raising capital, and what investors expect before they commit.
    • Investment readiness: numbers, documents and governance.
    • Expansion, partnerships and market access.
    • Compliance, due diligence and transactions.
    • The next phase of growth, with people who have been through it.

    Edition 1: a sold-out start

    The first Mixer was on 5 August 2026 at Santé Spa Cuisine in BKC, from 4 to 6 pm. We co-hosted it with Global St. Angelo’s Venture Funding and Vis Legis Law Practice. It sold out, with founders, investors and advisors in the room.

    Edition 2: on to Goregaon

    The second edition was announced for 2 September 2026 at Santé Spa Cuisine, NESCO Centre, Goregaon, with a refreshed format and the same curated guest list.

    It was set to bring together Agnelo Rajesh Athaide, Chairman of the Global St. Angelo’s Group of Companies, with two of our co-founders, CA Manan Patel and Adv. Rahul Hingmire, for views from entrepreneurship, investment, business strategy and law. Several outlets carried the announcement.

    Advice for founders from our co-founders

    Ahead of Edition 2, both shared what they most want founders to get right.

    Rahul Hingmire, on the legal side: “Legal readiness should begin from Day 1.” Sound documents with employees, customers, investors, vendors and banks, along with proper due diligence, protect what a company is worth. They also make raising money, scaling and later transactions smoother.

    Manan Patel, on money: make cash flow and runway a standing item, and open every board meeting with them. Founders watch growth closely and often leave cash until it is the only problem left. Runway is the clearest measure of the time a team has to build, change course or raise.

    Next: Edition 5

    The Mixer is becoming a regular meeting place for founders and business leaders in Mumbai. Edition 5 is set for 2 December 2026, back at Santé Spa Cuisine in BKC. Registration and later dates are under upcoming events.

    Great ideas, good advice and the right capital belong in one room.
