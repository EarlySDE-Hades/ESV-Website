    ElectriQ

    ElectriQ (Only Electriq Solutions Pvt Ltd) is a Hyderabad-based EV mobility OEM founded in 2021 by Anand Thakur. The company focuses on India’s gig and last-mile delivery workforce, a segment where vehicle cost, downtime and range anxiety directly affect riders’ daily earnings. ElectriQ manufactures electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers and small commercial vehicles at its plants in Hyderabad and Haryana, and wraps them in a full-service ecosystem: battery swapping and leasing with two-minute swaps, a digital platform for fleet, battery and rider management, ride rentals, roadside assistance and skill development. It works with delivery aggregators and has partnered with Yamaha Motor’s leasing arm in India to supply vehicles to food delivery fleets. ElectriQ’s aim is to make electric mobility the most practical and economical choice for every delivery rider.
