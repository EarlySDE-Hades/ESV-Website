    Flying Wedge Defence & Aerospace

    Flying Wedge Defence & Aerospace (FWDA) is a Bengaluru-based defence technology company founded in 2022 by Suhas Tejaskanda. The company designs and builds indigenous unmanned combat aircraft, with the aim of reducing India’s reliance on expensive imported platforms and giving the armed forces cost-effective autonomous airpower. Its FWD-200B, a medium altitude, long endurance (MALE) unmanned bomber with surveillance payloads and precision strike capability, completed its maiden flight in 2024 and is being taken forward with the Indian Army through the Army Design Bureau. FWDA has also unveiled the AI-powered Kaal Bhairava autonomous combat aircraft and is developing an AI-driven fighter jet. By building the full stack in India, from airframe to autonomy, FWDA is working to position the country among the leaders in next-generation unmanned warfare.
