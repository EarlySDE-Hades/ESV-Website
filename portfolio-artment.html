    The Artment

    The Artment is a contemporary home décor and lifestyle brand founded in 2019 by Aditya Agarwal and Aanchal Agarwal. Its collections of wall décor, lighting, dining, tableware and furniture draw inspiration from art movements such as Cubism, Impressionism and Abstract Expressionism, making artistic, design-led living accessible to Indian homes. What sets The Artment apart is Art Lab, its proprietary data analytics platform, which tracks evolving consumer preferences and guides which products to design and launch. Every piece is made in India through a deep supply chain spanning key craft and manufacturing clusters, including Moradabad, Khurja, Firozabad and Jodhpur, supporting local artisans. The brand sells through its own website and leading marketplaces, and is expanding into pop-up stores and retail partnerships as it builds a globally relevant lifestyle brand from India.
