    When founders come to us

    The meeting is booked. The questions are coming.

    • “An investor asked for projections by next week.”

      Projections with assumptions you can defend in the meeting.

    • “The deck looks fine but doesn’t land.”

      Structure first, design second.

    • “I don’t know what investors expect to see.”

      Learn the fundamentals first-time founders are expected to know.

    Why Prologue

    Built from the investor side of the table.

    Prologue is part of Earlyseed Ventures, an early-stage venture capital firm in Mumbai. Our team evaluates early-stage companies, so the work is shaped by what investors actually ask.

    • You own the work. You should be able to explain every assumption without opening the file.
    • Specific about what gets tested. The second meeting is usually where the model gets tested.
    • Honest about limits. A better deck won’t raise the round on its own. It removes reasons to say no.

    How it works

    Done with you, not for you.

    1. 1

      Discovery call

      Where your raise stands, what investors have asked and what you need first.

    2. 2

      Fixed scope

      Clear deliverables, turnaround and revision rounds, agreed before work starts.

    3. 3

      Working sessions

      Short, scheduled sessions so the deck and model reflect how you think about the business.

    4. 4

      Walkthrough

      We take you through every slide and assumption until you can defend them yourself.

    Who it’s for

    Founders in India preparing to raise.

    • First-time founders
    • Pre-seed startups
    • Seed-stage startups
    • Bootstrapped founders
    • Accelerators and incubators

    What Prologue does not do

    • Guarantee funding, or promise investor introductions.
    • Change the numbers to tell a better story.
    • Affect any investment decision by Earlyseed Ventures.

    Prologue is a paid preparation service. Working with Prologue has no bearing on any investment decision by Earlyseed Ventures, and information you share with Prologue is not used in ESV investment evaluation without your written consent.
