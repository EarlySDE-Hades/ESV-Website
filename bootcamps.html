    Bootcamps

    From the classroom to the pitch room.

    We connect with aspiring student entrepreneurs, and give them access to funding, mentorship and resources.

    Online and offline sessions taken by the Earlyseed Ventures team and industry experts, followed by an expert visit to campus for the final pitch day.

    • Market research
    • Go-to-market strategy

    • Governance, risk and compliance
