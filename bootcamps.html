    Bootcamps

    Founder bootcamps on campus.

    We connect with aspiring student entrepreneurs, and give them access to funding, mentorship and resources.

    • Business planning
    • Pitch decks
    • Financial projections
    • Valuations
    • Mentorship
    • Access to investors

    Highlights

    • MIT World Peace University, Pune

      A programme for 4,000+ students, covering everything from business planning to pre-funding exercises such as pitch decks, financial projections and valuations.

    • Gharda Institute of Technology

      An MoU to support its incubation centre with mentorship, bootcamps and workshops, industry insights and access to investors and partners.

    • Skill Titans

      Knowledge partners for OLL’s Skill Titans, in partnership with CNBC-TV18, alongside MIT Pune, Manipal University, Gharda Institute of Technology and Ashoka University.

    • IIT Madras

      Invited as mentors for student founders.

    • Manipal Academy of Higher Education

      University · Manipal, Karnataka · Est. 1953

      MAHE, an Institution of Eminence deemed-to-be university, spanning medicine, engineering, management, design and the sciences.

      manipal.edu

    • MIT World Peace University

      University · Pune

      Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, a private university in Pune with programmes across technology, management, design, law and the liberal arts.
