    OLL: a 15-year-old founder and the skills school leaves out

    Shreyaan Daga started OLL at 15. A ₹1 Cr raise came first, then Shark Tank India.

    Shreyaan Daga was 15 when he started OLL in Mumbai in 2020. The idea came from a gap he could see from inside a classroom: schools teach the syllabus, and leave out many of the skills people need once they leave. Coding, robotics, finance, languages, public speaking and the arts were things students had to find for themselves.

    OLL set out to fill that gap with live, hands-on courses. It runs a marketplace that connects students with verified teachers, and today it offers more than 600 new-age skills, for individual learners and for schools.

    The first raise, in 2021

    Early-stage edtech was a crowded space, and a founder still in school is not the profile most investors expect. In 2021 we raised ₹1 Cr for OLL. That round gave the company the runway to build its catalogue of courses and its network of teachers.

    On Shark Tank India

    OLL then took its pitch to Shark Tank India, Season 2, and secured ₹30 Lakh from Peyush Bansal and Vineeta Singh. For a young company, a national audience and two well-known investors on the cap table meant credibility with parents, schools and later investors.

    Where OLL is now

    OLL has reached tens of thousands of students across India, and runs free skill-development programmes for children from underprivileged backgrounds. It is building a nationwide network of schools and educators to make practical, career-relevant learning a standard part of every child’s education. Read OLL’s profile.

    What founders can take from it

    • Investors back the founder first. At the earliest stage, background and mindset weigh as much as the numbers. Age matters less than clarity about the problem.
    • One round can open the next. A well-run first raise gives a company something to show when the next opportunity, or the next investor, comes along.
    • Visibility is an asset. Public moments, like a national TV pitch, build trust with customers as well as investors.
