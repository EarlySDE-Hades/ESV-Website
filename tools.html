    Tools for founders and investors

    Quick, free calculators for the questions that come up in every raise. In rupees, in lakh and crore, and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

    Indicative only, and not financial advice. Always check with a legal advisor before you sign anything.

    For founders

    • Round finder

      Seven yes-or-no questions to find which round you are really raising, and what it usually raises in India.

      Open the tool

    • Pre-money and post-money calculator

      Enter what you are raising and the valuation. See the investor’s stake, the share price and how many new shares you issue.

      Open the tool

    • Dilution simulator

      Add up to four rounds and an ESOP pool, and watch every holder’s percentage, and the value of the founders’ stake, change.

      Open the tool

    • Runway calculator

      Cash in the bank, monthly costs and revenue: how many months you have left, and the latest date to start raising.

      Open the tool

    • Exit waterfall

      Sale price, investors and their preferences: who gets what when the company is sold, and when investors convert.

      Open the tool

    • ESOP pool sizer

      Size your option pool from your hiring plan, and see what it costs the founders at this round.

      Open the tool

    • Vesting schedule

      A grant, a start date and a cliff: how many options are vested on any date, and what they are worth.

      Open the tool

    For investors

    • Return calculator

      Cheque size, exit value and years held: the multiple and IRR, after later rounds dilute you.

    • Pro-rata cost

      What it costs to keep your percentage in the next round.
