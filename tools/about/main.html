About us

With founders, end to end

We support companies end to end, and onboard them with the vision of giving them future exits.

  1. 01

    Bootcamps

    We guide founders on round structuring, governance and risk management, go-to-market strategy, and other critical early-stage mentorship needs.

  2. 02

    Pre-funding

    We help founders build proper resources and structures for raising funds: market research, pitch decks, financial projections and valuation reports, through Prologue by ESV.

  3. 03

    Investments

    We curate deals for our investors and believe in strategic capital: sourcing through our network, validating through our in-house investment committee and due diligence, and negotiating valuation and terms.

  4. 04

    Investment banking

    We guide early-stage founders on round structuring and fundraising, give portfolio companies access to institutional investors and angels for subsequent rounds, and assist with deal execution.

  5. 05

    Exits, pre-IPO and acquisitions

    Our newest vertical, so that the companies we back early have a path to an exit.

Our growth journey

    Began our association as regional partners for a venture capital fund.

    Earlyseed Ventures Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated to support start-ups through investment banking services.

    Established an angel investor syndicate and carried out investments under the Earlyseed Ventures banner.
