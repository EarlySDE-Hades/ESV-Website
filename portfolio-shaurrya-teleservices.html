    Shaurrya TeleservicesShaurrya Teleservices

    Shaurrya Teleservices is a Gurugram-based digital infrastructure company founded in 2021 and registered as a Category I Infrastructure Provider. It follows a neutral-host model, building shared infrastructure that multiple telecom operators can use, which lowers deployment costs and speeds up network rollout. Its services cover telecom tower deployment, in-building solutions (IBS), fibre-to-the-home (FTTH), 5G-ready deployments, IoT and AI-enabled network platforms. Shaurrya is among the first eight agencies empanelled by TRAI as a Digital Connectivity Rating Agency, which allows it to assess and rate the digital connectivity of residential and commercial properties, and then help developers improve those ratings with its infrastructure solutions. A member of the Digital Infrastructure Providers Association (DIPA), Shaurrya is working to make India’s buildings and cities ready for 5G, 6G and IoT.
