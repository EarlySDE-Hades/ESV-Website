    Strategic Growth Partner

    Subramanian Srinivasan

    Founder, CEOMITRA

    Mr. Subramanian Srinivasan (Subuu) has helped four mid-sized European companies set up in India over a 32-year corporate career. At 31 he founded a startup and sold 1 lakh shares for ₹3.6 crore within 18 months. In 2021 he founded CEOMITRA, which helps organisations build self-driven, highly efficient teams. He is a certified business coach and Independent Director (IICA).

    Through CEOMITRA, Subuu helps organisations unleash the power of their people by building self-managed, self-driven teams in a happy workplace. Passionate about growth, Subuu goes by the name “Scale-Up Buddy”.
