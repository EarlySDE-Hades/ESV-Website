    OLL

    OLL is a Mumbai-based edtech company focused on the skills that traditional schooling often leaves out. Founded in 2020 by Shreyaan Daga, who started the company at 15, OLL operates a marketplace that connects students with verified teachers for experiential courses in coding, robotics, languages, arts, life skills and other new-age subjects. The platform works with both individual learners and schools, using technology to bring quality skill-based learning to students regardless of where they live. OLL has reached tens of thousands of students across India and has run free skill-development programmes for underprivileged children. The company raised funding on Shark Tank India Season 2 and is building a nationwide network of schools and educators to make practical, career-relevant learning a standard part of every child’s education.
