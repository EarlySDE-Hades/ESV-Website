    EsportsXO

    EsportsXO is a Bengaluru-based esports company founded in April 2020 by Utsav Umang, Rohit Raj and Vikas Goel. It brings together gamers, tournament organisers, game publishers and brands on a single ecosystem. Its products include BattleXO, a fully automated SaaS platform that lets any organisation host tournaments of any scale, along with a tournament discovery marketplace for players. EsportsXO also runs competitive teams, manages a roster of gaming content creators with large followings, and operates XO Space in Bengaluru, one of India’s first gaming houses, with streaming rooms, content production and residential facilities for players. For brands, it creates targeted engagement with India’s young gaming audience. In 2024, EsportsXO set up a regional headquarters in Abu Dhabi to expand into the Middle East and North Africa.
