    Hesa

    Hesa is a Hyderabad-based rural commerce company building an integrated marketplace for Bharat. Co-founded by Vamsi Udayagiri and Hema Nandiraju, it combines a technology platform with a network of trained village-level entrepreneurs, known as Hesaathis, who serve households in their own communities. Rural commerce still runs on trust and personal relationships, and Hesa’s phygital model is built around that reality. Through its super app, agri, fintech, e-commerce and consumer brands reach rural customers directly, while farmers can buy inputs and sell produce, and families can access banking, insurance and everyday products at their doorstep. Present in over 50,000 villages, Hesa aims to connect India’s 650,000+ villages to mainstream markets, giving businesses a reliable last-mile channel and rural households better access, choice and income opportunities.
