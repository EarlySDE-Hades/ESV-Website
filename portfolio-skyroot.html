    Skyroot Aerospace

    Skyroot Aerospace is a Hyderabad-based launch vehicle company founded in 2018 by Pawan Kumar Chandana and Bharath Daka, both IIT alumni and former ISRO scientists. Skyroot designs and builds the Vikram series of rockets to provide affordable, on-demand launches for the fast-growing global small satellite market. In November 2022, its Vikram-S became the first privately built Indian rocket to reach space on a suborbital flight. On 18 July 2026, Vikram-1, a carbon-composite orbital launch vehicle, lifted off from Sriharikota and reached low Earth orbit on its first attempt, making India one of only three countries with private orbital launch capability. Skyroot became India’s first spacetech unicorn in 2026 and builds its rockets at its Infinity Campus in Hyderabad, designed to produce one orbital rocket a month.
