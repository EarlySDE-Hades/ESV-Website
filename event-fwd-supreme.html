    An AI-piloted fighter aircraft takes the stage

    On 13 October 2026 our portfolio company Flying Wedge Defence & Aerospace unveiled FWD Supreme in New Delhi: the full-scale static prototype of an AI-piloted, fighter-class aircraft.

    Event
    FWD Supreme launch, Flying Wedge
    Where
    New Delhi
    Our part
    Portfolio launch

    Four years in the making

    Suhas Tejaskanda founded Flying Wedge in 2022. Four years later, an aircraft designed and engineered in India stood in front of the room.

    Built to decide for itself

    FWD Supreme is not a remote-controlled drone. It is designed to fly and make decisions using AI, and to fight in a coordinated swarm, under what the company calls its Mobbing Doctrine.

    What we take from it

    Deep tech and defence companies are rarely built in a single round. They need conviction, patience and the right people around them. That is why we back founders who set out to build something nobody has built before.
