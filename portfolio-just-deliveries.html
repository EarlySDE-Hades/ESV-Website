    JustDeliveries

    JustDeliveries is a Mumbai-based cold chain logistics startup founded by Mansi Mahansaria to bring structure and reliability to the movement of perishable goods. India loses a large share of its food to gaps in storage and distribution, and most logistics operations remain unorganised. JustDeliveries focuses on mid-mile cold chain transport for the food and beverage sector, serving quick-service restaurants, café chains and fresh and frozen food brands. Its asset-light, plug-and-play third-party logistics model, backed by its own technology stack, lets brands scale temperature-controlled distribution without building their own fleets. The company works with over 100 F&B brands, including household names such as ITC, Swiggy and Blue Tokai, and operates across Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad, with all hubs profitable at city level and further expansion under way.
