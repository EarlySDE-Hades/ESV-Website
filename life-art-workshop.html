    Life at Earlyseed

    2025

    Start before the picture is clear

    One weekend we gave the team a blank canvas each, and a simple brief: pick up a brush and make something abstract.

    What
    A weekend art workshop
    Who
    The whole team

    The messy middle

    It begins with an empty space. Then comes the part where nothing looks right and you cannot tell where it is going. Many of us recognised that from our twenties and thirties: confusing, sometimes too much, and full of possibility.

    Keep moving

    The canvases came together because people carried on. Taking the next small step made the one after it clearer. When we stood back, no two looked alike.

    What stayed with us

    • Act, even in small steps.
    • Trust yourself.
    • Let yourself be inspired.
    • Go where the work leads.

    After the paint dried

    We sat down and talked about what we want to build as a company, and how we want to be while we build it. It was an honest conversation, and we found we agreed on a great deal.

    You do not need the whole picture to begin.
