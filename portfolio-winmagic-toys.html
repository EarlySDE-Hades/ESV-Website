    WinMagic ToysWinMagic Toys

    WinMagic Toys is a Mumbai-based multi-brand kids’ merchandise company founded in 2017 by Mukesh Jagwani. It works on both sides of the toy business. For international brands, WinMagic is a go-to partner for entering India, handling marketing and distribution across modern retail, specialty toy stores and e-commerce. The company has introduced several new categories to Indian shelves, bringing in trend-setting lines such as Hatchimals and L.O.L. Surprise!, and has built a portfolio of over 40 brands. Alongside distribution, WinMagic designs and manufactures its own range of licensed and generic toys, made in India for domestic and international markets. In a market still dominated by unbranded imports, WinMagic is building a company with two strong divisions: third-party distribution and owned brands sold globally.
