    Garuda Aerospace

    Garuda Aerospace is a Chennai-based drone technology company founded in 2015 by Agnishwar Jayaprakash. It runs an integrated business spanning drone manufacturing, Drone-as-a-Service (DaaS) and DGCA-approved remote pilot training. Its indigenously designed Kisan drone was among the first in India to receive both DGCA type certification and remote pilot training approvals, and the company has since expanded into defence, surveillance, mining, energy and public safety applications. Garuda operates manufacturing and indigenisation facilities near Chennai, including a dedicated defence drone facility that covers design, prototyping, production and testing. Backed by cricketer MS Dhoni, who also serves as its brand ambassador, the company holds an export licence and is expanding into international markets, supporting India’s goal of self-reliance in drone technology.
