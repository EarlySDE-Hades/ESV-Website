    Life at Earlyseed

    We are a small team in Mumbai that works with founders at the very start: shaping the pitch, checking the numbers and finding the investors who fit.

    See our openings Intern with us

    What the work is like

    You work close to founders
    A raise is months of work, side by side: the story, the model, the data room and the conversations with investors.
    Your ideas count
    Our meetings used to end with task lists. Now they end with 15 to 20 new ideas on the board. How that changed
    No two companies look alike
    Our portfolio runs from space-tech and drones to rural commerce, esports and toys.
    You learn by doing
    Pitch decks, financial models, valuations and term sheets. You work on real ones, alongside people who have done it before.
    You get out of the office
    We run bootcamps on campus and events for founders and investors, and you will be in the room.
