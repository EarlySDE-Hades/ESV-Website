    CA Monica Gupta

    Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer

    Ms. Monica Gupta is a multifaceted entrepreneur and strategic advisor with a career across venture capital, corporate consulting, and business operations. She brings a rare blend of financial acumen, advisory, and leadership to every initiative she undertakes. She is also an active angel investor.

    Based in Mumbai, Monica has spent more than a decade helping new and mid-sized companies set up robust business practices on strong foundations. As co-founder of Earlyseed Ventures, she supports young entrepreneurs through their fundraising journeys, and she has invested as an angel in more than 18 startups.

    She has been a juror at pitch-fests hosted by IIT Powai, NMIMS, the Indian Dental Association and EvolveX Accelerator, among others, and sat on the jury of Skill Titans, the student entrepreneurship show. She has also hosted a panel of established investors at MIT’s RIDE event. A Rotarian, she has been closely involved in social projects in and around Mumbai.
