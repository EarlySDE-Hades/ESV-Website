    Join our syndicate

    Back early-stage start-ups alongside other investors. Our team screens each opportunity and runs diligence before it reaches you.

    From ₹5 Lakh
    The minimum syndicate ticket. Investors come in together, on the same terms.
    Screened first
    Start-ups are evaluated by our team before you see them, across sectors from space-tech and drones to mobility and rural commerce.
    Full documentation
    Diligence findings, an investment memo on the business, the round and the risks, and the legal paperwork.
    Learn as you invest
    Sessions on evaluating start-ups, valuations and term sheets, and a community of investors to compare notes with.

    How it works

    1. Tell us about yourself

      Share a few details about who you are and how you like to invest.

    2. Deep dive

      We learn your investment philosophy and strategy.

    3. Knowledge sharing

      We share our view of the startup ecosystem, then present curated start-ups to look at, meet and potentially invest in.

    4. Closing the loop

      If you commit, we carry out the procedures to close the investment and lay out the next steps. You decide deal by deal.

    Become a Strategic Growth Partner

    Our Strategic Growth Partners (SGPs) are industry leaders who have joined hands with us to mentor founders, open doors and build alongside us.

    Mentor founders
    Share what you have learned running, funding or advising businesses with the people building the next ones.
    Open doors
    Introduce start-ups to the customers, industry contacts and investors you know.
    Build alongside us
    Work with our team as companies grow, from early mentoring to fundraising and strategic exits.

    How it works

    1. Introduce yourself

      Tell us about your background, your network and the region you know best.

    2. Meet the team

      A conversation with our co-founders about what you would like to do and where you can help most.

    3. Agree how we work

      We settle the role and the terms together, one to one.

    4. Orientation

      Sessions on how we source, evaluate and support start-ups, so you can begin with confidence.
