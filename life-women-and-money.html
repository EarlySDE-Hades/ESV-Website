    September 2026

    A room full of women, talking about money

    One weekend in September 2026 our co-founder Monica Gupta spent a few hours with a group of women on a subject that rarely gets an honest conversation.

    What
    A session on financial literacy for women
    Led by
    Monica Gupta

    The questions

    There were no budgeting tips. The talk was about how we feel about money, who taught us about it, and who makes the financial decisions at home.

    What came up

    Many in the room had learned about money by watching somebody else handle it. Most leave insurance, tax and investments to another person. Nobody said it was because they could not understand it. They had simply never been invited in.

    The worksheet

    Everyone then placed their own money on a grid: active or passive, easy or hard to turn into cash. That is where the room stopped being polite, and the real learning started. There was a lot of laughter.

    Why it matters

    Financial independence is a safety net. It gives women choices, and a voice at the table. This session was the first of more.
