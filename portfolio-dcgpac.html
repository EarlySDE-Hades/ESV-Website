    DCGpacDCGpac

    DCGpac (DCG Tech Ltd) is a Gurugram-based packaging and procurement solutions company with roots going back to 2005. Founded by Suresh Bansal, DCGpac began by solving a simple problem: businesses could not easily buy quality packaging in the quantities they needed, when they needed it. Today it is a one-stop packaging partner, offering corrugated boxes, courier bags, protective packaging, tapes, labels and food-grade materials, along with packaging design and consultancy. Its ProPac suite covers sustainable packaging, automation, packaging-as-a-service, reusable and rental packaging, and circular-economy programmes. DCGpac runs a smart manufacturing plant in Noida, a pan-India fulfilment network and an AI-enabled sourcing platform, and has operations in the UAE and UK. The company aims to become a global, sustainability-driven packaging and supply chain leader built from India.
