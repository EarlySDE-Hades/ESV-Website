    RegenerixRegenerix

    Regenerix is a women-led climate-tech company, incubated at IIT Kanpur, that tackles one of India's biggest sources of air pollution: the burning of crop stubble. Every year, millions of tonnes of residue are set alight across Indian farms after harvest. Regenerix offers farmers another way out. Using its patented stubble removal technology, it collects the residue straight from the fields at no cost to the farmer and converts it into biofuel pellets on-site. Those pellets give industries a cleaner, cost-competitive alternative to coal, cutting emissions at both ends: in the fields where stubble would have been burned, and in the boilers where coal would have been fired. The company is led by founder and CEO Aanchal Jain.
