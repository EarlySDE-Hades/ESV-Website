    Cup-Ji

    Cup-Ji (Cup-Ji Foods and Beverages Pvt Ltd) is an Indian beverage brand built around a simple idea: a great cup of tea with nothing to prepare. Each Cup-Ji cup contains a whole-leaf tea blend made with real herbs, spices and botanicals, sealed at the bottom of a paper cup beneath a filter mesh and an airtight layer that keeps it fresh. Users simply add hot water, swirl and sip. The cup design is protected by a granted Indian patent. Cup-Ji offers more than 23 flavours across green and black teas, from Jasmine and Lemon Ginger to its signature Chatkara Green Tea. The brand comes from a family business with decades of experience in tea exports, has featured on Shark Tank India, and sells through its own website, marketplaces, corporate pantries and international trade channels.
