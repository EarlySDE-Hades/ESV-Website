    CA Manan Patel

    Co-founder and Chief Investment Officer

    Mr. Manan Patel is a Chartered Accountant and seasoned financial strategist with extensive experience in investment structuring, tax advisory, and regulatory compliance. He is also an active angel investor.

    With over 14 years of experience, he has advised high-net-worth individuals, small and medium enterprises, Non-Resident Indians and corporate clients. His expertise covers tax structuring, FEMA compliance, transfer pricing, direct and indirect tax strategy, and tax-related litigation.

    More recently, he has focused on advising startups on financial projections, valuations and tax structuring: areas that are often overlooked, but integral to their long-term success.
